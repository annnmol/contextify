Fun Splash Zone – Small water park with slides & fountains
Playground Hub – Swings, slides, climbing frames
Huddle Sports Corner – Group games & mini tournaments

For All Ages (Families & Teens)
Jogging & Cycling Track – Dedicated clean pathways
Yoga & Meditation Lawn – Peaceful green spaces
Community Event Area – Weekend activities & performances
Open Fitness Zone – Bodyweight workout equipment

Open Gym – Pull-up bars, cycles, benches, and more
Morning Fitness Groups – Yoga, Zumba, stretching sessions
Running Track – Ideal for daily cardio routines
